<h2 align="center"><a href="https://cityreply.com">→ cityreply.com</a></h2>

<p align="center">Permits for San Francisco projects, worked by AI agents.</p>

- **Do I need a permit?** Tell Civic, our AI agent, your address and what you plan. CityReply checks it against the city's own permit data and says what the city will ask for.
- **The paperwork, with you.** CityReply prepares the application with you, and follows it through city review.
- **The city decides.** City staff make every permit decision; CityReply never does.

The app and its agents: [CityReply/cityreply](https://github.com/CityReply/cityreply)
